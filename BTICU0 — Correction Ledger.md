# BTICU0 — Correction Ledger

Sep 25, 2026 · @Gregory Broadbent

## Purpose and method

This ledger records, claim by claim, what in the prior series is falsified, erroneous, unsupported, or standing. Every later BTICU paper cites it rather than restating inherited results. As of 30 Sept 2026, all prior material (IR, UV, K, MU series and Framework C) is classed as research notes, not publications; publishable work will be developed within BTICU.

The method follows the UV117 Appendix (24 Sept 2026):

1. State the claim as the source paper makes it, with its location.
2. Trace every input. A result inherits any problem in its inputs.
3. Fix the prediction before looking at the target value.
4. Test against neighbouring alternatives (other levels, other ratios) chosen in advance.
5. Compute how often chance matches as well, and report the null result when that is the outcome.

Categories: **Falsified** (existing measurements exclude it) · **Error** (arithmetic, units, or mislabelling in the text) · **Unsupported** (no independent derivation; fitted or asserted) · **Stands** (correct and independent of the above) · **Open** (a defined calculation not yet done).
